<details><summary>Answer</summary>

> The carbon removal module (33, default realization `portfolio`) uses the carbon price in one place. It uses it to cap how much a region spends on net-negative emissions, measured against its GDP.
> 
> **Where the price enters**
> - `modules/33_carbonRemoval/portfolio/equations.gms:313-317` holds `q33_carbonRemovalspending`:
>   ```
>   v33_NetNegEmi_expenses(t,regi) =e= (1-cm_frac_NetNegEmi) * pm_taxCO2eqSum(t,regi) * vm_emiAllco2neg(t,regi)
>   ```
> - `pm_taxCO2eqSum` is the total CO2 price. It combines the general trajectory, the regional markup and other components, and it comes from module 46 (carbonpriceRegi).
> - `vm_emiAllco2neg` is the net-negative CO2 emissions, entered as a positive number. It comes from module 21 (tax).
> - The equation is only active for `t.val ge max(2035, cm_startyear)`.
> 
> **What it limits**
> - `v33_NetNegEmi_expenses` is the implied expense for net-negative emissions: price × net-negative emissions, scaled by `(1 - cm_frac_NetNegEmi)`.
> - `modules/33_carbonRemoval/portfolio/postsolve.gms:10` sets its upper bound each iteration:
>   ```
>   v33_NetNegEmi_expenses.up(t,regi) = p33_GDP_NetNeg_share(regi) * pm_gdp_gdx(t,regi)
>   ```
> - The result is that net-negative emissions priced at the carbon price can't exceed a share of regional GDP.
> - That share comes from `cm_33_GDP_netNegCDR_maxShare`. It is set in `datainput.gms:148-151`. The default is `GLO` = 1, meaning 100% of GDP, and it can be set per region.
> - At that default, the cap rarely binds. It only matters if you lower the switch.
> - `cm_frac_NetNegEmi` is the fraction of net-negative emissions that is not counted toward the expense. At 0.5, half of the priced amount counts.
> - The equation's own comment warns that it needs adapting if `cm_NetNegEmi_calculation = 1` is used.
> 
> **Other limits in the module**
> - The module's other limits don't use the carbon price. These are the final-energy share cap (`cm_33_maxFeShare`) and the enhanced weathering (EW) and ocean alkalinization (OAE) limits.
> - The biochar revenue uses a separate material-value price (`cm_33_BCpriceForm`), not the carbon price.
>
